What is a carbon footprint?

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases emitted into the atmosphere, this could be carbon dioxide(CO2), methane (CH4), nitrous oxide (N2O), or hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs). Someone's personal carbon footprint can be determined as the total amount of greenhouse gases produced by our personal actions, this can include transportation, household activities, or even clothing and food. Read more about carbon footprints at: What does a carbon footprint mean

Why should you try to lower your carbon emissions?

Climate change is affecting the entire globe, and is causing extreme weather events such as flooding, extreme heat waves, heavy downpours, as well as rapidly changing climate conditions. Our carbon emissions heavily affect climate change which is why we believe in green energy and the reduction of carbon emissions.
