Energy usage

A persons energy usage is heavily determined by the amount of electricity and gas that they use, many factors can affect your energy usage, including:

  • The size of your home
  • The energy efficiency of your home
  • The amount of people who live in the home
  • The energy efficiency of your household items and how often you use them

Read more about energy usage here: Energy advice

How to reduce energy usage?

The more energy you use, the more money you pay, reducing your energy usage can be quite simple, turning all electronics and lights off when not in use, switching any incandescent lightbulbs to LED, use power strips for multiple devices, switch to energy-efficient appliances, and more.
